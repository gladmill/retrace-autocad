# Evidence: pictures for the sign-off sheet

`steps.xlsx` is a sign-off sheet (`recording-format.md`). Its Evidence column (I) is where a tester shows what they saw. Retrace fills it in two ways. Neither needs a macro-enabled workbook, and neither changes `steps.xlsx`: both write a **new** workbook beside it, `steps-evidence_<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss>.xlsx` (a second run makes a second file, never overwrites the first).

## A person walks the test: the Evidence window (`RETRACEEVIDENCE`)

Open it from the Open test window (Evidence (camera)) or type `RETRACEEVIDENCE`. Nothing else in Retrace lists the steps of a test for a person to walk (the Guide palette shows the guide's pages, the replay window only shows steps during a replay), so this window is that list.

- The drop-down chooses the test (newest first). The list is the rows of its sign-off sheet (`1`, `1.1`, `2` ...), the current one highlighted; Previous step / Next step move it.
- **Camera** (the button, or `Ctrl+Shift+F12`): every window of Retrace's own that is open (the Evidence window, Open test ...) and a floating Guide palette are hidden (a docked Guide palette is part of AutoCAD's layout and stays), AutoCAD's window is brought to the front, the picture is taken (AutoCAD's window on its monitor's work area, so palettes and dialogs of AutoCAD are in it and the task bar and Retrace's windows are not, scaled to at most 1280 px on the long side), and the windows come back without taking the keyboard focus from AutoCAD: a tester in the middle of a command keeps typing into it. The hotkey is handled only while the Evidence window is open (the note in I3 of steps.xlsx says so) and the key reaches AutoCAD (AutoCAD's own message loop; a key pressed in another program is that program's, and a modal dialog that runs its own loop may not see it: use the button). There is no setting for it: it is not an AutoCAD shortcut. If your checkpoint shortcut (setting `checkpointKey`) is the same chord, the checkpoint wins and the window says so. While recording, the chord is not counted as your input. The Open test button for Evidence closes Open test first.
- Several pictures per step are fine. Retake takes another picture and removes the step's last one only once the new one is on disk; Delete picture removes the picked one. A removed picture is moved to `evidence\removed\`, not deleted.
- Pictures are files: `<test folder>\evidence\step-<row>_<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss-fff>.png` (`step-2.1_20261117-101530-042.png`).
- Tester, Date and, per step, Result and Notes are the tester's to fill in the window (kept per test while the window is open, so switching tests and back loses nothing; Refresh reads the list of tests again). A step's Result is written only when set to Pass, Fail or Blocked; otherwise it stays "Not run" for Excel. Closing the window forgets them (the pictures stay on disk). A picture belongs to a step by its number: when steps.md has changed since the first picture of the test was taken (the test was recorded again into the same folder, steps.md was edited) the window says so in red.
- **Write workbook** writes the new workbook. Each step's pictures sit in the Evidence cell of its row with the sheet's own rule (420 px wide, at most 380 px high, 4 px inset, aspect kept, never enlarged), stacked top to bottom with 4 px between; the row grows to hold them (at most 60 pictures of one step go into the workbook; a note says how many stay in the folder). Excel does not allow a row above 409 pt, so when a stack is taller its pictures all shrink by the same factor, down to half size; beyond that the rest go to the next column (`Evidence 2`, `Evidence 3` ...) in the same row. Tester and Date go in their cells, Result and Notes where set, and the Overall result and counts follow the Result column as in steps.xlsx. Retrace's own picture column is unchanged.
- The how-to note in I3 of steps.xlsx tells the tester about the camera and keeps the manual way as the fallback (Win+Shift+S, click the cell, Ctrl+V; a pasted picture does not size itself, which is why the camera is the easier way).

## Retrace replays the test: evidence from a replay

Off by default. Two ways to ask for it:

- the Open test window's **Run with evidence** button (same as Run this test, no verdict box);
- `-RETRACEEVIDENCE` on the command line (scripts): asks for the test (a name, a test folder, a path to its `recording.jsonl`, or `last`).

The replay is the one RETRACERUN does (new drawing, dialogs filled in). After every step has run and AutoCAD has settled (700 ms, then AutoCAD must have been idle since, at most 3 s more; then every window of Retrace's own and a floating Guide palette are hidden for 200 ms and shown again in the same call) a picture of AutoCAD's window is taken. (Retrace's windows are hidden for each picture, including the replay window; the "Step n/m" text at the right end of AutoCAD's status bar in those pictures is the AcadStatus add-in's status pane, part of AutoCAD's window, not Retrace's replay window.) When the test ends the workbook is written in the background and its path printed on the command line:

- **Evidence** = the picture after that step (a step with dialogs owns the picture after the last of them). A test that ends early (error, stop for a person) gets a picture of what AutoCAD showed then as the picture of the step that was being run; a Stop pressed by a person takes none.
- **Result** per step: Pass when all its replay steps ran, Fail when one failed, timed out or ended the run with an error (or a checkpoint of it failed), Blocked when the replay needed a person there or hit a security prompt, Not run when the replay never got to it, was stopped by a person there, or a checkpoint of it had no verdict. The sub-rows (`2.1`) of a step carry its result. A step the replay has no step of (a drawing switch) takes the result of the step before it when the replay ran to the end. **Notes**: the failure message (a checkpoint: its differences), the block, "Checkpoint not verified." and, for a step where the replay waited for a person at a dialog, "A person finished a dialog here". Messages are shortened to 400 characters. What is scrubbed: in every message that reaches the workbook (Notes, checkpoint differences, the end check line, the verdict message) and in the Info sheet's "Test folder" line of an evidence workbook, a path that starts with a drive letter and `Users\<name>` (backslashes or forward slashes) is written as `%USERPROFILE%`. Nothing else is changed: a tests folder on another drive or a network share is written as it is, and the plain steps.xlsx Info sheet is as it was.
- **Overall** never reads Pass unless the replay's verdict is PASS: a failed end check (basic check) makes the last step Fail with the differences in its Notes; a run that completed but verified nothing (verdict INCOMPLETE) leaves the last step Not run with the note "Replay ran every step; nothing verified the result (verdict INCOMPLETE): a person must check", so Overall shows In progress.
- Tester reads `Automated run (Retrace)`, Date is the day. The Info sheet has a line `Run` ("Automated run by Retrace <version> on AutoCAD <version>, <date and time>. Nobody clicked anything ...": when a dialog had to be finished by a person it says so instead) and the verdict and message of the replay. No user name and no computer name is written.
- Pictures are in `<test folder>\evidence\auto_<yyyyMMdd-HHmmss>\step-NN.png` (NN = the replay's step), apart from a person's.
- How a replay step is matched to a sheet step: both have the time of their first event in the recording; a replay step belongs to the last numbered step of the script that began at or before it (a dialog a command opened belongs to the command). If the script and the recording disagree on the number of steps the Info sheet says so.

A suite or Tdoc run never takes evidence pictures: each of those has its own timing (a step timeout, picture points), and a picture per step would change what they do. Replay one test at a time for evidence. No other replay changes: without the evidence request the replayer holds no capture and none of the hooks does anything.

## Limits

- Not tested in real Excel (only the OpenXML structure is checked in the unit tests, and the sheets were opened on the test machine without Excel).
- The camera captures the screen, so another program's window over AutoCAD would be in the picture; the replay brings AutoCAD to the front before each picture.
- An automated run's pictures are as accurate as the replay is: a dialog that Retrace could not fill in stops the run for a person (Blocked).
